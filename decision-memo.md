# Project 2 decision memo

**Reject adoption for now. Reconsider when fresh validation under the same mandate identifies a mix whose allocation sensitivity and estimated net performance can be defended.**

The student-confirmed seven-year mandate uses VTI, BND and SGOV, a 6.5% estimated annual return floor, at least 3% in SGOV, full investment and long-only positions. The 60% VTI and 40% BND mix is a diagnostic benchmark. The frozen adjusted-price snapshot spans September 30, 2021 through September 30, 2026. The baseline uses 60 monthly returns and a five-percentage-point allocation grid.

| Case | VTI / BND / SGOV | Annual return estimate | Annual volatility | Grid verdict |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Full window | 30% / 0% / 70% | 6.54% | 4.85% | Feasible |
| Last 36 monthly returns | 15% / 0% / 85% | 6.94% | 1.97% | Feasible |
| Full window with 25 bp annual cost | 35% / 0% / 65% | 6.76% net | 5.64% | Feasible |

The full-window search finds 72 feasible mixes out of 231. The minimum-volatility feasible allocation shifts up to 15 percentage points across estimation windows and 5 points under the existing cost diagnostic. Those shifts support the decision to defer adoption despite grid feasibility. The cost stress uses a flat incremental drag. Historical estimates and grid checks leave live trading costs, liquidity and issuer look-through unresolved.
